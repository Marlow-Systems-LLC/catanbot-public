# The Great Canal (Der grosse Kanal) - official rules

Scenario 5 of "Treasures, Dragons & Explorers" (German: "Schaetze, Drachen & Entdecker"). Played with Seafarers + Cities & Knights. Researched 2026-10-04. All wording is paraphrased.

Primary sources (both read in full for this scenario, texts agree):
- [DE] German rulebook, catan.com: https://www.catan.com/sites/default/files/2021-06/CATAN_SchaetzeDrachenEntdecker_Spielanleitung.pdf (scenario 5 on PDF pp. 7-8, booklet pp. 13-14)
- [EN] English translation of the German rulebook (R. Hyde): https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/23/0f/1b-catan-treasures-dragons-explorers-rulebook.pdf
- [CK] C&K 2020 rulebook: https://www.catan.com/sites/default/files/2021-06/catan_c_k_2020_rule_book_200708.pdf
- [GAME] Catan Universe local strings (read earlier, see docs/great_canal_rules_research.md): resources.assets in the Steam app 544730.
- [STEAM] https://steamcommunity.com/app/544730/discussions/0/1639787494950905885 (player discussion, not official)

General rule (DE/EN p.1): base game + Seafarers rules apply to all scenarios; for C&K scenarios the C&K rules also apply, with the combined Seafarers/C&K rules in the C&K book (section on the seafaring game). Scenario rules override. The DE book says the scenario first appeared in Catan News 2003 and is in several digital versions.

## 1. Board / setup (DE, EN)
- 3 or 4 players.
- Farmland and mountain hexes are placed as on the printed map. Desert "A" (the western basin) comes from this pack; its reverse is sea. Sea hexes are placed and the small-island hexes go into the gaps.
- Light-brown area: remaining land hexes laid out randomly: 4 brick, 5 wool, 4 wood.
- Number chips of the 2 gold-river hexes and the farmland hexes go on as printed; the farmland chips are then turned face down. While desert A is not flooded the farmland produces no grain.
- Other chips random; red numbers must not touch each other.
- Harbours and the "Catan" VP chips are placed as printed (map-only detail; exact positions not in text).
- Small islands exist; one gold-river hex (2 in total on the map) and harbours sit on them. East lake "B" is the water source; it does not count as coast.
- Material of the pack: 9 canal tiles (straight and curved), 12 number chips, cities in 4 colours.

## 2. Setup order and starting pieces (DE, EN)
- Everyone starts with 2 settlements + 1 city, each with one adjacent road or ship, all on the home island.
- All players first place a settlement, then a city in place of the second settlement. Base-game order applies (second round goes in reverse).
- The last player to place a city places their second settlement (with road/ship) immediately after it; then the other players place their second settlement clockwise.
- Starting resources: each player gets the resources of the hexes around their last-placed settlement, including grain from a barren farmland. The city gives no start cards.
- Limits: every player needs at least one settlement or city on the coast (the east lake is not coast); a coastal settlement/city requires a ship (not a road).

## 3. Canal (DE, EN)
- Who digs: knights, called "canal builders". Knight strength is irrelevant.
- Trigger: as soon as 2 ACTIVATED knights stand on intersections of a hex that carries Catan chips, a canal is dug at once (on activation of the second knight; no third player can step in).
- Effect: the 2 Catan chips are removed; a canal tile (trench side up) is placed on the hex, turned so it meets an adjacent canal tile or adjacent Catan chip. Straight or curved tile chosen as the orientation requires.
- Cost: no resource cost stated; only the two activated knights (activation itself costs 1 grain as in C&K).
- Knights are NOT deactivated after the dig.
- Victory points: each Catan chip = 1 VP. If both knights belong to one player, that player takes both chips (2 VP). If they belong to two players, each takes one chip (1 VP each). (Universe labels these "Canal Points".)
- Completion: the canal is finished when the 9th-minus-1 = 8th (penultimate) tile is built. If the 9th was not built at the same time (possible when an activated knight stands on a crossing between two chip hexes), it is laid now. The 2 leftover chips leave the game. All canal tiles flip to water side; desert A and the farmland chips flip face up; grain is produced again from then on.

## 4. Gold (DE, EN)
- Gold is collected only by knights ("gold prospectors"), not by settlements.
- A player with a ship line reaching an intersection of a gold-river hex on a small island may move a knight onto it. While it stands there, the player gets any 1 resource of their choice whenever an 8 is rolled. The knight need not be active.
- Two knights on the same gold hex still give only 1 resource.
- When the canal is finished (desert flooded), small-island knights lose the prospector function.
- No settlements on small islands at all, so gold corners cannot be built on.

## 5. Ships / pirate (DE, EN)
- Pirate: none; only the robber is used.
- Ship lines may NOT be branched in this scenario (overrides Seafarers).
- Other ship rules (cost, movement, open-end only) are standard Seafarers / C&K seafaring rules (not restated in the scenario).
- A coastal start piece requires a ship (see section 2).

## 6. Robber, barbarians, progress cards
- Robber: starts on any desert (DE, EN). No pirate.
- "Robber moves only upon/after the first barbarian attack": stated in Catan Universe strings [GAME]; this is the standard C&K rule (robber and pirate stay put until the first attack) [CK]. Not restated in the scenario text.
- Friendly robber: not part of the scenario text. Universe has a generic option (tooltip: robber may not go next to a player with only 2 VP) [GAME]; the owner's table uses 4 VP. Not official scenario rule.
- Barbarians: standard C&K, but knights standing on the small islands do NOT count for defence (DE, EN).
- Progress cards: only change stated is Irrigation ("Bewaesserung"): it is carried out even if the affected farmland is still barren (DE, EN). Inventor, Deserter, Merchant etc.: no scenario change stated; standard C&K.

## 7. Knights (DE, EN)
- Knights may be built only on the home island.
- They are moved to the small islands via ship lines: gold prospector (above) and trader.
- Trader: a knight moved onto a harbour intersection of a small island lets the owner use that special harbour; activation not required.
- Small-island knights are excluded from barbarian defence.
- (Home-island knights otherwise follow normal C&K activation/move rules.)

## 8. Victory
- 21 VP with 3 players, 18 VP with 4 players, reached on your own turn (DE, EN). Confirmed in Universe strings [GAME].
- No special VP besides the Catan chips from canal digging (each chip is a VP).

## 9. Piece limits / other differences
- No scenario-specific piece limits are stated; standard C&K/Seafarers limits apply.
- Differences from standard C&K+Seafarers: barren grain until the canal is done, knights gain extra jobs, no pirate, no ship branching, no settlements on small islands, Irrigation works on barren fields, small-island knights ignored in defence, start with 2 settlements + city, 18/21 VP.

## Catan Universe notes
- Menu name: "(C&K+Sf) The Great Canal"; no pirate; 21/18 VP on own turn; canal points tooltip; desert chips upside down until canal done [GAME].
- Cardboard and Universe agree on everything checked. No Universe patch note or wiki page found describing differences.

## Unconfirmed / conflicting
- Exact hex/harbour/gold/chip positions: only in the book's map image, not extractable as text.
- Whether Universe uses cardboard setup order for round 3 exactly; not documented.
- Friendly-robber threshold (2 VP generic tooltip vs 4 VP owner's table): unofficial.
- Master Merchant ignoring Canal Points in VP comparison: single player bug report [STEAM].
- Ships on lakes: [STEAM] poster says not forbidden but not done in practice; the rulebook only says the lake is not "coast".
- Searches for BGG threads, Catan Universe forum and patch notes returned nothing usable (BGG/Reddit not reachable via search); Fandom wiki returned HTTP 402.
