# Catan Universe: The Great Canal (rules research)

Researched 2026-10-02. Catan Universe's own in-game rule text is not publicly indexed, so the core rules below come from the physical board game rulebook for the same scenario (Catan: Treasures, Dragons & Explorers). Catan Universe's implementation may differ in details. Items not confirmed for Catan Universe are marked **[UNCONFIRMED]**.

Primary source for the scenario (English translation of the Kosmos rulebook): https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/23/0f/1b-catan-treasures-dragons-explorers-rulebook.pdf

## 1. The Great Canal

### Where it sits in Catan Universe
- Confirmed: it is one of two scenarios in the Catan Universe special scenario pack, (https://store.steampowered.com/app/544730/Catan_Universe/; https://www.apkmirror.com/?p=2040187).
- Printed: "Scenario 5, for 3 and 4 players", played with Seafarers and Cities & Knights together (rulebook above, p.13).
- Steam players call it "The Grand Canal" in places (https://steamcommunity.com/app/544730/discussions/0/1639787494950905885).

### Board layout (printed rules)
- Home landmass in the west with a desert basin "A" (desert tile, sea on its reverse). A high mountain lake "B" is in the east. Farmland and mountain hexes sit as in the diagram. The remaining land (4 brick, 5 wool, 4 wood) is randomly placed in a marked zone.
- Small islands sit in gaps in the sea, including two Gold River hexes. Ports are at fixed positions.
- Number tokens on the two Gold River hexes and the farmland hexes are fixed. The farmland tokens are turned face down, so **farmland produces no grain until the canal is finished**. Other tokens are random, with no adjacent red numbers.
- 9 canal tiles (straight and curved) are the special pieces.
- Exact hex coordinates are in the rulebook diagram only. They are not extracted here. **[UNCONFIRMED] for the Catan Universe layout.**

### Setup (printed rules)
- Everyone starts with **2 settlements and 1 city**. Place a settlement first, then a city instead of the second settlement, each with a road or ship, on the home island. Start-order rules are as in the base game. The last player to place a city also places their second settlement straight away, then the others place theirs clockwise. Starting resources come from the last-placed settlement.
- Each player needs at least one settlement or city on the coast. The eastern lake does not count as coast. A coastal settlement must be paired with a ship.

### How canals work (printed rules)
- **Knights** may only be built on the home island.
- **Canal builder**: when 2 activated knights stand on the intersections of a hex that has a Catan chip, a canal is dug right away.
  - The two Catan chips are removed and a canal tile goes on the hex, aligned so it links to an adjacent canal or chip.
  - If both knights belong to one player, that player takes both chips. If they belong to different players, each takes one. **Each Catan chip is worth 1 VP.**
  - Knight strength does not matter, and the knights stay activated.
  - The build happens immediately on the second activation, so no third player can step in.
- **Completion**: with 9 tiles in total, the canal is finished when the penultimate tile is built. The last tile is then placed too. The two chips left are removed from the game. All canal tiles flip to their water side, and the desert and the farmland tokens flip back. **From then on grain is produced again.**
- **Gold prospector**: a knight at an intersection of a small-island gold hex, reached by a ship line, gives 1 resource of choice when an 8 is rolled. The knight need not be activated. Two knights on the same hex still give just 1. This stops once the desert is flooded.
- **Trader**: a knight at a harbor intersection on a small island lets you use that port. It need not be activated.
- Knights on small islands do not count toward barbarian defense.

### Other rules (printed rules)
- Only the robber is used, with no pirate. It starts on any desert.
- No settlements on the small islands.
- Ship lines may not be branched in this scenario.
- The Irrigation progress card works even while farmland is barren.
- Players on the Steam discussion say ships cannot be built on the lakes, and that Master Merchant plus Canal Points was buggy at one point (https://steamcommunity.com/app/544730/discussions/0/1639787494950905885). Anecdotal.

### Scoring and victory points (printed rules)
- **21 VP for 3 players, 18 VP for 4 players**, on your own turn. Catan chips from canal building count 1 VP each, on top of the usual Cities & Knights scoring.
- Catan Universe's targets are **[UNCONFIRMED]**.

### Differences from base Catan
Seafarers ships and gold, Cities & Knights (knights, barbarians, commodities, progress cards, 2006 seafaring combination rules), a start with a city, grain locked until the canal is finished, canal-chip VP, and island knights as prospectors and traders.
