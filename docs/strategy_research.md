## 1. Seafarers (ships and gold, as used in The Great Canal)

Sourced material is thin. Rules (not strategy) are confirmed; strategy items are mostly [G].
- Ships cost 1 wood + 1 sheep; place next to a coastal settlement or an existing ship; end ships of a route can be moved [S: https://www.catan.com/game/catan-seafarers-expansion].
- First settlement on a new island gives a bonus VP (scenario-dependent) [S: same; https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/325/catan-seafarers].
- Gold field: 1 resource of choice per settlement, 2 per city [S: Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catan:_Seafarers].
- Expansion priority: reach the coast early; ships double expansion options; longest route plus new-island VPs decide games [S: BGG summary, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/801292/3-different-strategies-leads-to-a-close-game].
- [G] Gold hexes with 5/6/8/9 are top-value openings (any resource, fixes scarcity); gold next to a settlement is worth more than a mediocre resource hex.
- [G] Race for the nearest unclaimed island before opponents; do not extend a ship line with one ship per turn if blocking can strand it.

## 2. Cities & Knights

Opening
- First settlement on ore marquee numbers (5/6/8/9); ore is needed for knights, upgrades, metropolis [S: UBG-CK].
- Secure wheat so the first knight can be built and activated before the first barbarian attack [S: UBG-CK].
- Aim a city next to a commodity hex (paper/cloth/coin): only cities produce commodities, which gate every improvement [S: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2166619 via search summary; UBG-CK]. Owner strategy says paper > coin > cloth and city on commodities (memory file).
- Get 3+ cities early for commodity advantage [S: search summary of Great Board Games guide, https://www.greatboardgames.blog/catan-cities-and-knights-guide/].

Knights and barbarians
- Knights must be activated (1 grain) to count in defence; many beginners forget [S: search summary above].
- Build/activate the first knight before barbarians land or lose a city [S: UBG-CK].
- Keep at least 2 active knights; being the weakest defender loses a city [S: search summary above]. Owner rule: 1 active knight per city (memory) - consistent with "knights proportional to city count" [S: https://www.ultraboardgames.com/catan/cities-and-knights-game-rules.php summary].
- Place knights adjacent to where the robber is likely to go and on future settlement sites [S: UBG-CK].
- Once your army is strong you get a defender point each barbarian visit [S: UBG-SC]. Knights are deactivated after each attack, so reactivation costs grain every cycle [G].
- Mighty knights need the Fortress (politics level 3) [S: https://ultraboardgames.com/catan/cities-and-knights-strategic-considerations.php search summary].

Improvements and metropolis
- Pick one track and go deep; level 4 then 5 gives a metropolis (+2 VP, cannot be pillaged) [S: search summary, BGG tactics thread https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2166619].
- Flip the first page of all three tracks early; green/science (Aqueduct: free resource on a no-yield roll) is strongest, blue/trade weakest per UBG; others argue Trading House [S: UBG-CK; https://ultraboardgames.com/catan/cities-and-knights-strategic-considerations.php].
- Science first, then compare; Alchemist/Inventor accelerate metropolis fights [S: UBG-CK].
- Metropolis is a "virtual point" along with longest road; plan 11 VP quietly then jump 2 in one turn [S: UBG-CK, UBG-SC].
- Merchant Guild (trade 3?) allows 2:1 commodity trades [S: strategic-considerations search summary; exact level [?]].
- Three city walls raise hand limit to 11 (walls: +2 each) [S: UBG-SC].

Trading / robber
- Never give commodities to players who have not flipped that track page [S: UBG-CK].
- Keep ore for yourself; wheat trades well [S: UBG-CK]. Hold access to all five resources by mid-game to avoid embargo [S: UBG-SC].
- Robber cannot be moved until the first barbarian attack [S: UBG-CK; also the official rule].
- Destroying an opponent's first city (barbarians, Diplomat, Deserter/Intrigue) hurts most [S: search summary of BGG thread].

Progress cards
- Watch rivals for Resource Monopoly, Trade Monopoly, Commercial Harbour [S: UBG-SC].
- [G] Priority: Alchemist (set dice for a city), Inventor, Irrigation/Mining, Warlord, Deserter/Intrigue on a leader, Diplomat.

## 3. Seafarers scenario: The Great Canal (C&K + Seafarers)

Rules (official, TDE; 3-4 players)
- VP target: 21 VP (3 players), 18 VP (4 players) [S: TDE]. The brief said 19 VP for 4-player; the rulebook says 18. Verify what the app shows. [?]
- Setup: each player starts with 2 settlements and 1 city (settlement first, then a city instead of the 2nd settlement; last player places city, then second settlement); at least one on the coast (the lake does not count), coastal placements need a ship [S: TDE]. Start resources come from last-placed settlement, including grain from barren farmland [S: TDE].
- Robber only (no pirate); starts on a desert [S: TDE].
- Knights may only be built on the home island [S: TDE].
- Canal digging: when 2 activated knights stand on intersections of a hex that carries Catan chips, the canal is dug immediately: both chips removed, canal tile placed [S: TDE]. Both knights yours: you get both chips (2 VP); different owners: 1 chip (1 VP) each. Knight strength irrelevant; knights stay activated [S: TDE]. Cannot be intercepted by a third player because it triggers on the second activation [S: TDE].
- 9 canal tiles; canal completes at the penultimate tile (last tile placed then, remaining chips removed), tiles flip to water, desert and farmland number tokens flip, grain produces again [S: TDE]. Until then the farmland (grain) hexes produce nothing [S: TDE].
- Gold prospector: knight at an intersection of a small-island gold hex (reached by your ship line) gives any 1 resource on an "8"; knight need not be active; 2 knights still give 1 [S: TDE].
- Trader: knight at a small-island harbour intersection gives that harbour without activation [S: TDE]. Island knights do not count for barbarian defence [S: TDE].
- No settlements on small islands; after flooding, gold prospectors stop working; do not branch ship lines; Irrigation works even on barren farmland [S: TDE].
- Strategy: third settlement bordering a grain hex lets you get the first active knight up and benefits from Irrigation always [S: STEAM]. Gold via knight beats relying on ports or Merchant cards [S: STEAM]. Roads between canal hexes + knights on them give steady canal VPs [S: STEAM]. Consider cities on brick and trade brick for grain [S: STEAM]. Ships cannot be built on the lakes [S: STEAM, player claim].
- Why a 3:1 port matters (inference, not sourced): grain is unavailable until the canal is done, but knights (1 grain + 1 ore... knight needs wool+ore, activation needs grain) and city growth need grain, so a port converting surplus to grain is key; the gold prospector knight on an "8" and the harbour knight are alternatives. [G]
- Bot encoding ideas [G]: pair-up knights on a canal hex so the second activation is yours (both chips = 2 VP) rather than leaving a hex where an opponent can pair; do not activate your knight on an empty canal hex where an opponent's other knight is already waiting if you want to deny sharing... but sharing gives 1 VP each, so prefer solo double-dig. Canal VPs are finite (about 18 chips across 9 tiles) so the race ends when the canal completes.
