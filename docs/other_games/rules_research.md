## 1. Greater Catan

### Where it sits in Catan Universe
- The task brief says Catan Universe lists it under Multiplayer > Cities & Knights. **[UNCONFIRMED]**: no web source found that says so. The Steam store page lists only Enchanted Land and The Great Canal as scenarios (https://store.steampowered.com/app/544730/Catan_Universe/).
- In the printed rulebook it is "Scenario 3, for 3 and 4 players", a Seafarers scenario that can also be played with Cities & Knights (rulebook above, p.7-9).

### Board and players (printed rules)
- Players: 3 or 4 (rulebook above). Catan Universe's player cap for this mode is **[UNCONFIRMED]**. Catan Universe has a 5-6 player extension for the base game, Cities & Knights and some Seafarers scenarios (https://www.catan.com/catan-fans/news/catan-universe-5-6-player-extension-new-rule-now-available), but I found nothing tying it to Greater Catan.
- Board: a home island plus a ring of "new islands" in the surrounding sea (rulebook above).
  - Home island: 14 land hexes randomly placed in the 3-player game; built per base-game rules in the 4-player game.
  - New islands: 20 hexes (3 players) or 26 hexes (4 players), mixed and placed randomly face up.
  - Reserve number tokens: 4 (3 players) or 5 (4 players), face down in a supply stack.
- Catan Collector describes the outline as a wide 6x9 board (https://catancollector.com/greater-catan). It also says the scenario was in the 1st-edition Seafarers rulebook and is playable in the Catan Mac game.

### Victory points
- Seafarers only: **18 VP**, checked on your own turn (rulebook above; https://catancollector.com/greater-catan).
- Combined with Cities & Knights: **20 VP**, checked on your own turn (rulebook above, "Combining with Cities & Knights").
- The VP target used by Catan Universe for the Cities & Knights version is **[UNCONFIRMED]**.
- Standard Cities & Knights is 13 VP (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catan:_Cities_%26_Knights).

### Rule differences from standard Cities & Knights / Seafarers (printed rules)
All from the rulebook above, unless noted.
- **Cities**: each player gets 4 extra cities, so up to 8 cities.
- **Impoverishment rule** (the main twist): when you place a road or ship next to a hex with no number token, you must put a token on it, taking one from the reserve. Once the reserve is empty, you must take a token off the home island instead. Constraints, in priority order:
  1. 6 and 8 may not be adjacent on the small islands.
  2. You may only take a token from a hex adjacent to one of your own settlements or cities.
  3. Every settlement or city on the home island must keep at least one numbered adjacent hex.
  If all three cannot be met, break them in the order 1, 2, 3.
- **Pirate and robber** are both in play. The robber starts on the desert. The pirate starts on the sea hex marked "x". In the 3-player game there is no desert on the home island, so the robber starts on the frame.
- **Setup**: the first two settlements go on the home island with roads. A coastal settlement may use a ship instead of a road.
- **Combining with Cities & Knights**: use the C&K rulebook's seafaring section (p.9 "The Seafaring Game", 2006 edition).
- Barbarians, metropolis, progress cards and commodities: the Greater Catan section changes none of these. They follow standard Cities & Knights, which has 3 commodities (paper, cloth, coin). Source for the commodities: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catan:_Cities_%26_Knights. I found nothing saying Catan Universe changes them. **[UNCONFIRMED for Catan Universe]**.

### Where I looked and found nothing for Catan Universe
Steam store page, Steam news (the 5/6 player post body would not load), SteamDB patch notes search, Steam community discussion 1639787494950905885 (no mention of Greater Catan), and two WebSearch passes. No Catan Universe page names "Greater Catan".

## 3. Other Catan Universe modes (brief)
Sources: https://store.steampowered.com/app/544730/Catan_Universe/, https://www.apkmirror.com/?p=2040187, https://www.catan.com/catan-fans/news/catan-universe-5-6-player-extension-new-rule-now-available, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catan:_Cities_%26_Knights

- **Arrival on Catan** (free tutorial/starter): a guided base-game start, with multiplayer up to 3 players and a single-player mode against the AI.
- **Base game**: standard Catan, 10 VP. Up to 6 players once unlocked. The 5-6 player rule replaces the old Special Building Phase (catan.com link above).
- **Seafarers**: ships, the pirate, gold and island scenarios. Up to 6 players for selected scenarios.
- **Cities & Knights**: 13 VP. Commodities (paper, cloth, coin), city improvements, knights, barbarians, progress cards and metropolises. Up to 6 players.
- **Rise of the Inkas**: a separate edition. Settlements decay as the jungle takes over and players rebuild. Exact VP and rules **[UNCONFIRMED]**. Only the Steam page and APKMirror summaries were found.
- **Special scenarios pack**: Enchanted Land and The Great Canal. The printed Enchanted Land rules say the C&K knights go to the island to fight dragons, and the game ends at 13 VP in the section I read. Catan Universe values **[UNCONFIRMED]**.
- **Catan: The Duel**: a 2-player card game (free intro, with paid full version and sets).
- **5/6-player scenarios**: added for the base game, Seafarers and Cities & Knights.
